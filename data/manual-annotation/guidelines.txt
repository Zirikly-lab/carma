Labels
True Positive (TP)
Based on what I just read, do I believe this person to have the mental illness mentioned in the “diagnosis” column? Is it being captured accurately by the algorithm and/or do they mention symptoms/medications in their post? They do not have to say “I OFFICIALLY got diagnosed” to classify as a TB.
True Positive Examples: 
انا عندي اكتئاب
اتشخصت ب اكتئاب

False Positive (FP)
A phrase could match the diagnosis but still not be considered a true positive because the hardcoded algorithm has shortcomings, for instance, sarcasm and talking about others. NOTE: We are not defining FP as a weak diagnosis. If you think a diagnosis is weak, this should be the next label.
اخويا عنده اكتئاب
الاكتاب هيموتني ههههههه

Need more information to tell (NA)
In case it IS a positive match with the algorithm but the post did not convince me the person has a mental illness. Please try your best to classify as FB or TB before using this label.
حاسس عندي اكتئاب
ممكن يكون عندي اكتاب
